# My Favorite Movie: *The Avengers* (2012)

**The Avengers** is my favorite movie because it's exciting, funny, and the heroes work together to save the world. I like the team chemistry and big action scenes.

## Why I Like It
- Classic superhero team-up.
- Good mix of action and humor.
- Strong characters with different skills.

### Main Characters (Avengers)
1. **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** — the genius billionaire with a suit.  
2. **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** — the brave leader with a shield.  
3. **Thor** — the powerful god thunder with his hammer.  
4. **Hulk (Bruce Banner)** — strong but struggles to control his anger.  
5. **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)** — smart and skilled spy.  
6. **Hawkeye (Clint Barton)** — the expert archer.
